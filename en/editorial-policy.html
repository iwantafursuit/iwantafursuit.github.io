Editorial policy

Our buying guides explain how to research a maker, compare offers and prepare a commission. Examples illustrate decisions. They should never be presented as quotes from a particular maker.

How we review listings

We request a public portfolio or official contact link, country, the types of work offered and permission to use any submitted images. When reviewing a listing or correction, we compare those details with the public sources supplied. A listing is a research lead; availability, terms and payment details should be confirmed with the maker.

Corrections and removals

Send the page URL, the affected field and a current source through our contact page. Report images or links that you believe are unauthorised, explicit or unrelated. Reports are reviewed before making updates, and directory profiles are excluded from advertising.

Commercial links and editorial review

A paid highlight is a promotional option for makers. It does not establish a recommendation. New guides are reviewed before publication for accuracy, attribution and usefulness. Where a price or material claim needs evidence, we check the primary source or omit the claim.
