Privacy policy

David Garcia Fernandez operates I Want a Fursuit as an individual based in Spain and is the controller of the personal data described here. For privacy requests, use the contact page to send a private message to @FursuitComision on X.

I Want a Fursuit publishes guides and a maker directory. The site does not offer on-site accounts or process commission payments. Search and filter values are processed in your browser and may appear in your address bar and browser history. Avoid entering private information.

Maker listings and photos

The Google Forms listing form asks for a name, short and long descriptions, one to five fursuit photos (up to 10 MB each), public contact details, country and a yes/no answer about whether you are an adult. David uses these answers to review the request and create the profile. If a listing is accepted, the directory can show the name, descriptions, authorised photos, public links and country. The answer about adulthood is used during review and stays off the public profile. The photos stay on the website until the maker asks to replace or remove them, subject to earlier removal if needed.

Google Forms processes the submission and uploads for the listing request. For publication, provide only details you want shown and images you have permission to share.

Email and stored copies

Google Forms automatically collects your email address when you submit a listing request. David uses it to manage or follow up on the request. Only David has access to the submissions he receives. The email collected automatically and the answer about adulthood stay private. He keeps copies of responses, messages or uploaded files in Google services or on his computer. The site’s backups can contain previous versions of public listings. Our retention schedule calls for deleting private submissions and unnecessary copies within 90 days after closing the request, and rotating dated site backups within 90 days of creation. This schedule applies to those copies. The published gallery keeps authorised photos while the listing is active and they remain necessary for it. Where needed, David keeps a separate, minimal record of permission, a legal obligation or a claim. You can use the contact page to ask about a copy or request deletion.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which can receive technical request data such as IP addresses. The site links to maker sites, Google Forms, X, Amazon and PayPal. These services apply their own privacy policies. Makers’ public links and directory information can be corrected or removed on request.

Optional services and requests

If enabled, Google Analytics only loads after an analytics opt-in. It receives the page path without query parameters and generic interaction events. If advertising is activated, reviewed articles may use a certified consent platform to manage Google advertising preferences. Contact the project through the contact page for privacy questions or directory corrections.

Your privacy rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing where applicable, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). AEPD
