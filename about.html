About I Want a Fursuit

I Want a Fursuit helps buyers find fursuit makers and prepare for a purchase. The directory groups listings by country and style and links to maker portfolios. The guides explain what to ask about fit, materials, quotes and care.

Using the directory

The maker and the buyer agree on the work, price and payment directly. Availability and policies can change. Read the maker’s current terms and ask how changes, delays, returns and repairs are handled.

Suggest a listing

Makers can submit their official contact links, examples they have permission to share and the types of work they accept. See our editorial policy for listing checks and correction requests.
