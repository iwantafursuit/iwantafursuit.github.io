Premade or custom fursuit?

A premade suit already has a design and may have set dimensions. Request actual internal dimensions where available, ask how the maker recommends checking fit, and confirm which parts are included in the sale. If you may add pieces later, ask if the maker can still source the materials or share matching information.

A custom commission starts from a design brief and can include specific fit or configuration requests. Ask which decisions affect price, what changes you can make after purchasing materials, and how the production queue works. Fit changes and delivery dates should be discussed in writing before payment.

Compare the costs you would actually pay

For a premade, include any approved tailoring, replacement pieces and shipping. For a custom order, compare the quote, deposit schedule, confirmed extras and shipping. Keep the model and date for each offer so you can revisit the decision if a listing sells or a quote expires.

Questions for either option

  1. Can I see the exact piece or a recent example of the same construction?
  2. What happens if a part arrives damaged or differs from the agreed scope?
  3. Can the suit be used and maintained the way I expect?

If the suit needs to be wearable at a specific event, check production, dispatch and fitting separately. Ask the maker what can realistically be promised and leave time to check the fit after delivery.
