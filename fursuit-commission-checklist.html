Fursuit commission checklist

Keep one copy of the agreement and a list of questions you still need answered. Use your maker's requirements when they ask for a specific file format, measurement technique or deadline.

Before requesting a quote

  • Attach a readable reference with front, side and back views where they matter. Point out deliberate asymmetry, including the character's left and right.
  • Name the pieces you need and list features such as removable padding, indoor or outdoor soles, or a specific closure.
  • Give the destination country and whether you have a fixed event date. Ask for an estimated completion window and a separate shipping estimate.
  • Ask for the maker's fitting instructions. A body measurement and a garment measurement can describe different things.

Before paying a deposit

  • Save the written scope, total and currency. Ask who pays processing fees or shipping and whether any quoted tax is included.
  • Confirm the identity and payment channel through the maker's independently located portfolio or contact page.
  • Ask what happens if you cancel, the maker cancels, a material changes or the expected delivery window slips.
  • Record approval points for changes to the reference or construction. Ask what changes remain possible after the maker starts cutting materials.

Before the parcel ships

Confirm your address through the agreed private channel. Ask whether the shipment is tracked, how damage should be reported and what packaging to keep if there is a problem. When you receive the suit, check each piece against the agreed list and raise any mismatch before wearing or altering it.

Example message. “I'd like a head, hand paws and a detachable tail. The reference shows different markings on the two sides. Could you quote those pieces, tell me how you'd check the head fit with glasses, and confirm which terms apply if the build or shipping window changes?”
